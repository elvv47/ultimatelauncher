Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Disclaimer. Ultimate Launcher is NOT an OFFICIAL launcher and has NO AFFILIATION with MICROSOFT or MOJANG. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang Synergies AB / Microsoft Corporation.

1. Summary

All user data, in particular your Microsoft Account data, is stored locally on each player's computer. We do not run servers that collect, store or sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your computer

  • Microsoft account sign-in data (such as account name and authentication tokens) used to log you into Minecraft;
  • Your builds, game versions, mods and launcher settings (theme, language, scale, background);
  • Game logs and the files you choose as a custom background or skin.

This data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by signing out in the launcher or by deleting the launcher's data folder.

3. Microsoft sign-in

When you sign in, authentication is performed directly between your computer and Microsoft and Xbox/Minecraft services. The developers of Ultimate Launcher never receive your password or tokens.

4. Network connections

To work, the launcher connects directly from your computer to third-party services, for example to download Minecraft files, mod loaders and Modrinth content, and to sign in. These services have their own privacy policies. We do not control them.

5. Optional features

  • Discord Rich Presence (can be turned off in Settings) shows the build you are playing in your local Discord client;
  • If you choose to send game logs to the developers, you do it manually and you decide what to share.

6. No sale or sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data, and we do not use advertising or tracking.

7. Children

Use of Minecraft and Microsoft accounts is subject to Microsoft's age rules. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
